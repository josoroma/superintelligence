# SuperIntelligence

Write the name as **SuperIntelligence**. The visual wordmark places the octopus between **Super** and **Intelligence**.

The mark follows the supplied neon octopus reference: a rounded organic head, two luminous eyes, eight tapered flowing arms, and small sucker details. A continuous contour moves from violet and magenta to electric blue and cyan. The surrounding icon tiles and connector nodes are omitted. All artwork is editable SVG with transparent backgrounds and no font dependencies.

Wordmark ink: `#192842` / `#F5F8FF`. Contour: magenta `#E679FF`, violet `#BD52FF`, periwinkle `#7474FF`, electric blue `#28B8FF`, and cyan `#53E0FF`. The dark body gives the color mark contrast on light backgrounds.

The standalone symbol has a 128 × 84 viewBox. Keep at least 8 units of external clear space. Use the symbol alone below 240px wordmark width; prefer 96px or larger when showing the sucker details. The color identity uses self-contained gradients and a restrained vector glow. Monochrome versions use filled silhouettes with transparent eye and sucker cutouts, without gradients or filters. The collection’s icon SVGs retain their existing flat styles.

Edit [identity-octopus.mjs](../src/identity-octopus.mjs) for the symbol or [scripts/brand.mjs](../scripts/brand.mjs) for the font-free lettering and placement. Rebuild with `node scripts/brand.mjs` or `npm run build`.
