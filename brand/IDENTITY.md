# SuperIntelligence

Write the name as **SuperIntelligence**. The visual wordmark places the robotic-brain octopus between **Super** and **Intelligence**.

The exposed brain represents intelligence; the optical housing and eight articulated metal arms represent perception, tools, and coordinated action. The artwork follows the supplied robotic octopus reference, with a split coral cortex, blue camera optics, and segmented steel limbs. All details are editable vectors with transparent backgrounds and no font dependencies.

Wordmark ink: `#192842` / `#F5F8FF`. Steel: `#5F6C80`. Cortex: `#E597A9`. Optics: `#2B7EA6`.

Keep clear space of at least 10 units on the symbol's 80-unit grid. Use the symbol alone below 240px wordmark width; use 64px or larger to show its mechanical detail. Monochrome versions use vector masks for transparent seams and cortex folds. Edit [identity-robot.mjs](../src/identity-robot.mjs) and [identity-arms.mjs](../src/identity-arms.mjs), then rebuild with `node scripts/brand.mjs` or `npm run build`.
