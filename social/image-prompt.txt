SuperIntelligence social launch card

1600 × 900 landscape. Dark navy background, exact neon octopus wordmark, crisp sans-serif typography, restrained violet and electric-blue accents. Headline: “Icons for the AI stack.” Use the collection's actual native SVG geometry for the nine-icon gallery: agent, multi-agent, llm, rag, tools, gpu, memory, mcp, monitoring. node social/generate-launch.mjs
node scripts/render-svg.mjs social/superintelligence-launch.svg --out social/superintelligence-launch.png
